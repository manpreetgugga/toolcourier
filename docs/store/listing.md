## Full description

Tiny Courier is a relaxing 3D puzzle game about a little walking mailbox with parcels to deliver. Turn tiny floating islands, find the way, and bring every parcel to its pad. No timers, no lives, no rush: just calm logic puzzles to play at your own pace, offline.

HOW TO PLAY
Each level is a small floating diorama made of soft clay. Drag to turn the island and pinch to zoom, tap a spot and the courier walks there, and carry each parcel to the pad with the same colour and symbol. When every pad is filled, the flag at the exit goes up and you're on your way. The first level walks you through it, one step at a time.

The orange parts move. Slide a bridge into place, ride a lift, press a button to open a gate, hop on a conveyor belt or turn a crank to swing a turntable around. Work out the order, and the way opens: a gentle brain teaser in every level.

460 LEVELS ACROSS SEVEN WORLDS
• Meadow: hills, stairs, sliding bridges and rafts
• Harbour: lifts, gates, buttons and conveyor belts
• Old Town: two parcels at once and turning bridges
• Orchard: wooden planks that hold for just one crossing
• Blossom Valley: spring gardens where every moving part comes together
• Sunflower Fields: springs that bounce you over walls and gaps
• Frost Peaks: the final climb, over thin ice that cracks and breaks behind you

Each world's levels wind up a map of floating islands, with windmills, a lighthouse and a hot-air balloon to tap along the way. The puzzles start simple and grow slowly, so there's room to learn and still plenty to think about for puzzle fans. Every level has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
• No timers and no lives: a cosy, stress-free puzzle game
• Undo any move, or restart the level
• Stuck? A hint shows the next step of the shortest way to finish
• Earn up to three stars by finishing in fewer moves
• The puzzles play offline, with no account needed
• Your progress is saved on your phone

EASY ON THE EYES AND EARS
• Hand-pressed clay blocks, a dusk sky and a sea of clouds
• Every parcel and its pad share a symbol as well as a colour, so colour-blind players can match them too
• Calm music for each world, soft sound effects and light vibration, each with its own setting
• Graphics settings for older phones

ADS, KEPT CALM
The first 20 levels have no ads at all. After that, you can choose to watch a short video to turn on hints for a level, and now and then a short ad plays between levels. Never in the middle of a puzzle, and never banners.

If you like relaxing puzzle games, logic puzzles and little worlds to explore, Tiny Courier is a calm way to spend a few minutes, or a whole evening.
