# Play Store listing

## App name

Tiny Courier

## Short description

Calm clay puzzles: turn tiny floating dioramas and deliver every parcel.

## Full description

Tiny Courier is a calm puzzle game about a little walking mailbox with parcels to deliver.

Each level is a small floating island made of soft clay. Drag to turn it around, tap a spot to walk there, and carry each parcel to the pad of the same colour. When every pad is filled, the flag at the exit goes up and you're on your way.

The orange parts move. Slide a bridge into place, ride a lift, press a button to open a gate, hop on a conveyor belt or turn a crank to swing a turntable around. Work out the order, and the way opens.

FIVE WORLDS, 50 LEVELS
• Meadow: hills, stairs, sliding bridges and rafts
• Harbour: lifts, gates, buttons and conveyor belts
• Old Town: two parcels at once and turning bridges
• Orchard: wooden planks that hold for just one crossing
• Frost Peaks: ice you slide across until something stops you

Every level has been checked by a solver, so there is always a way through.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
• No timers and no lives
• Undo any move, or restart the level
• Stuck? A hint shows the next step of the shortest way to finish
• Earn up to three stars by finishing in fewer moves
• Your progress is saved on your phone

A GENTLE LOOK AND SOUND
• Hand-pressed clay blocks, a dusk sky and a sea of clouds
• Calm music for each world, soft sound effects and light vibration (you can turn them off)
• Graphics settings for older phones

ADS, KEPT CALM
The first world has no ads at all. From the second world on, you can choose to watch a short video to turn on hints for a level, and now and then a short ad plays between levels. Never in the middle of a puzzle, and never banners.

No account needed, and the puzzles play offline.
