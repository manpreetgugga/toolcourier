# Credits

Tiny Courier is made almost entirely in code. Below is every asset in the
game and where it came from.

## Font

| File | Asset | Author | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fonts/Nunito.ttf` | Nunito (variable weight), from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/nunito) | The Nunito Project Authors ([googlefonts/nunito](https://github.com/googlefonts/nunito)) | SIL Open Font License 1.1, in `fonts/OFL.txt` |

The font is used unchanged. The OFL lets it be bundled with the game and
used in it; it must keep its licence alongside and may not be sold on its own.

## Sound and music

All of the sound effects (`audio/sfx/*.wav`) and the five world loops
(`audio/music/world_<n>.wav`) are generated by `tools/make_audio.gd`, from
sine partials, filtered noise and a small reverb. No recordings, samples or
third-party sounds are used.

| Loop | Mood |
| --- | --- |
| `world_1.wav` Meadow | C major pentatonic kalimba over warm pads, a soft bass, the odd bird |
| `world_2.wav` Harbour | G mixolydian marimba over a low pad, waves and a far-off buoy bell |
| `world_3.wav` Old Town | Raga Bhupali on D: a tanpura drone with santoor phrases |
| `world_4.wav` Orchard | F major finger-picked guitar over a warm pad |
| `world_5.wav` Frost Peaks | E minor airy pads, sparse bells and the wind |

## Art

Every model, texture, shader, particle and animation (the clay blocks, the
courier, the props, the sky and clouds, the weather and the user interface
icons) is built procedurally in code under `render/`, `shaders/`,
`entities/` and `ui/`. There are no external model, image or texture files.
